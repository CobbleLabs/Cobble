# SilentAura protocol-space rotation and attack ownership repair

SilentAura now acquires targets in protocol space, publishes whole-GCD rotations, rebases from the final accepted wire look, and admits only its own nested swing and attack packets while it owns rotation. The earlier live-visibility, incoherent publication, and nested packet deadlock paths are resolved. The repair still cannot activate on raw-obfuscated runtimes because C0A is mandatory but has no raw class fallback. Build validation succeeds, but the runtime packet interleavings remain untested.

Watch for: **confirmed, blocking** — raw-obfuscated clients cannot resolve C0A, so `updateRotation` permanently leaves SilentAura inactive; **confirmed, non-blocking** — the repaired packet and GCD invariants have no focused automated coverage.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Target selection, aim geometry, reach validation, and block occlusion now use local `lastReportedPos` and remote fixed-point `serverPos`. Removing `canEntityBeSeen` avoids mixing render-interpolated visibility with protocol-space attack decisions.

The controller rebases from final yaw and pitch after competing packet owners run. Pitch is converted to whole mouse-GCD steps and bounded in step space before the `[-90, 90]` limit; publication revision and packet serial tie each attack to an accepted movement packet.

C02 and C0A packets are blocked during SilentAura ownership unless emitted inside its thread-local attack enqueue. **Confirmed, blocking:** C02 has explicit raw aliases, but mandatory C0A resolution has no equivalent alias, disabling SilentAura on raw-obfuscated runtimes.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

## Protocol-space acquisition replaces live render visibility

`selectTarget`, `updateRotation`, and `publishedRayHitsTarget` use local `lastReportedPos` and remote `serverPos / 32`. The selection loop no longer calls `canEntityBeSeen`; the final attack gate block-raytraces to the protocol-space AABB intersection. **Confirmed:** the prior target-acquisition blocker is resolved, and missing protocol fields reject selection or rotation instead of falling back to render coordinates.

## Final-wire rebase and whole-GCD publication

Accepted competing C03 packets are observed after higher-priority rotation owners modify them. A rotating packet updates `wireYaw_` and `wirePitch_`, requests a controller rebase, and invalidates attack publication. Pitch clamps its integer GCD count between `ceil((-90-managedPitch)/gcd)` and `floor((90-managedPitch)/gcd)` before applying the step. **Confirmed:** the prior rebase and bounded-pitch publication blockers are resolved.

The attack path snapshots publication validity, yaw, pitch, packet serial, and revision under `publicationMutex_`, checks geometry, then revalidates and holds the mutex through synchronous enqueue. **Confirmed:** this keeps C02 tied to the accepted C03 carrying the validated look.

## Nested C0A/C02 ownership avoids publication-lock recursion

`g_silentAuraAttackEnqueue` is set only around `mc.attackEntity`, admitting that call's nested swing and use-entity packets while rejecting unrelated C0A/C02 traffic during active ownership. Both nested packet types fail the C03 check before `onOutgoingPacket` attempts `publicationMutex_`. **Confirmed:** no new packet recursion or deadlock blocker was found, and ownership filtering is resolved where both packet classes resolve.

## Mandatory C0A resolution disables raw-obfuscated runtimes

**Confirmed, blocking:** `updateRotation` requires `resolveC02(env)` and `resolveC0A(env)` before activating rotation. C02 retries raw names (`ja` for 1.7.10 and `io` otherwise), but C0A only requests `net.minecraft.network.play.client.C0APacketAnimation`. `Java::AssignClass` translates failed MCP lookups through `notchClassName`, whose packet map contains C03 and C0D but not C0A. Raw-obfuscated runtimes therefore never publish or attack. Add verified per-version C0A raw aliases locally or add C0A to the central map.

## Runtime invariants lack focused regression coverage

**Confirmed, non-blocking:** no automated harness exercises raw C02/C0A resolution, unrelated attack suppression, nested owned packet admission, final-wire rebase, duplicate C03 publication, protocol-space acquisition, or pitch near `-90` and `90` across sensitivities. Add packet-gate and controller boundary tests. `build.ps1 -Build` completed successfully, validating compilation and integration but not these interleavings.
