NoJumpDelay

Remove the delay between jumps for bunny hopping.

Usage

Simply enable the module and hold SPACE to continuously jump. The module forces the player into ground state to bypass vanilla jump delay.

Keybinds

Bind any key or mouse button to toggle modules.

Supported Inputs

  • All keyboard keys
  • Mouse buttons: Left, Right, Middle
  • Side buttons: MB4 (Back), MB5 (Forward)

Usage

Click the keybind button next to any module and press the desired key. Press ESC to clear a keybind.
